Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-16
  Changes:
    - Rainbow scale setting from 1 to 5: 1 is one long rainbow over 24 tiles (as before), 5 is a tight rainbow every tile and a half.

    - Smaller rainbows are drawn with shorter pieces so every color shows.
